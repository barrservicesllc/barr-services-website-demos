## Physical advertising: separately scoped, end-to-end
The separate physical package covers **all six formats** shown on the proposal page:
1. Billboards and outdoor faces (media space rental, start/end dates, proof of posting)
2. Roadside and yard signs (site selection, landlord approvals, local codes, fabrication)
3. Truck/trailer graphics (measurements, proofing, qualified application, vehicle down-time)
4. Container branding/decal applications (Noll ownership/permission, surface prep, suitable materials)
5. Banners and fence/jobsite signs (site permissions, safe installation and removal)
6. Flyers/direct mail/postcards (design, print quantities, lawful distribution)

### Required delivery stages
1. **Scout:** evaluate traffic corridors, relevant local markets, availability, land/site permissions, billboard owner/vendor credibility and safety constraints. Document candidates and alternatives, not speculative placement guarantees.
2. **Design:** confirm actual Noll brand assets, legally approved copy, artwork dimensions, readable calls to action and production proofs; get approval per format.
3. **Source + produce:** request comparable itemized vendor quotations, material specifications, timelines and proofs; disclose who fabricates what and vendor margins if applicable.
4. **Place:** coordinate permitted installation with insured/qualified providers, landlord consent, municipal sign ordinances, highway/road-ad restrictions where relevant, scheduling, site access and removal obligations. No self-placement on public rights-of-way without authority.
5. **Verify + track:** proof photos, display dates, print counts, media invoices, dedicated call/QR metrics, lead-source notes, and lessons before renewals.

### Cost model
- **Service/creative fee:** scouting, research, design, supplier management, coordination, reporting (fixed scoped project fee or defined hourly cap).
- **Production/fabrication:** materials, printing, wrapping, sign hardware, quality checks, install preparation.
- **Media/placement:** billboard inventory rental, landlord site rental, distribution, permits, installer labor, travel, renewal and removal as applicable.
- **Client must approve:** every chosen placement, vendor, quote, written scope, creative proof, ad account access, expenditure and recurring commitment separately.

**Budget guidance:** Do not advertise a guessed billboard installation price. Scout exact locations and get real local outdoor-media/vendor quotations first. Signs and wraps have very different costs and constraints. Start with low-cost approved yard/container branding before committing to a premium billboard rental.

## Recommended targeting
Start with viable Leesport-area delivery routes and customers in construction, commercial/warehouse, homeowner storage and agriculture. ~250 miles is only a previous high-end planning assumption to verify with Noll's actual transport costs, equipment and carrier partners; no blanket delivery or price guarantee.

## Messaging / sample ad copy
- **Brand:** NOLL Container Rentals
- **Signature:** Storage space. Delivered.
- **General:** Need more storage? Ask Noll about container rental options for homes, job sites, businesses and farms.
- **Jobsites:** Keep tools and materials close to the work. Request a storage container quote.
- **Business:** Make room for inventory and seasonal peaks. Ask about available sizes and delivery options.
- **Call to action:** Request a quote / Ask about availability.

Do not imply rental rates, container quantities, a specific delivery radius, 24/7 service, real inventory or customer endorsements until independently approved. Do not claim Walmart as a client without explicit permission.

- Digital: impressions, frequency, relevant query volume, click-through, unique landing visits, qualified calls, quote inquiries, cost per qualified lead, quoted rentals and booked-rental contribution.
