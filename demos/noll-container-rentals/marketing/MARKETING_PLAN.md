## 3. What to do first (free-first)
1. **Official identity and media:** Dave approves name, logo, phone, public email, website, photos. Shoot 12–20 actual units and a 15-second real walkaround. Record permission from property/vehicle owners and people in the footage.
2. **Google Search and Maps:** Check whether an eligible Google Business Profile already exists. Claim or enhance *the correct existing business*; do not create duplicate profiles just to promote a separate demo name. A Profile is free. If Noll serves customers at their locations, follow service-area business policies; Business Profile service areas are specific cities/postal areas (up to 20), not mileage circles. Usually service-area boundaries are about 2 hours' driving time, with possible exceptions. If Noll has a staffed storefront open to customers, check the storefront/hybrid requirements before showing an address.
3. **Website SEO:** explain 10/20/40/high-cube options as inquiries, availability subject to quote, condition, delivery details, commercial or residential applications. Genuine regional pages only where service is actually available. Establish public domain and real operational quote intake before directing traffic.
4. **Offline recognition:** approved container-side decals, yard signage if allowed by property/zoning, optional hauler magnets/decals with permission, business-card/QR handout to contractors/restoration firms/warehouses and supplier contacts. Never print a QR leading to a demo quote form that doesn't deliver messages.
5. **Organic video:** four 10–20 second posts showing real doors/condition, walkaround, "What fits inside a 20-footer?" with measured verified dimensions, and steps to get delivery quote. Publish only with Dave's approval.

## 4. Paid ads, only after pricing/margin and routing approved
**First test:** Google Search Ads for high-intent queries. Keyword candidates:
- "storage container rental"
- "shipping container rental near me"
- "20 foot storage container rental"
- "40 foot storage container rental"
- "construction storage container rental"
- "jobsite storage container rental"
- "on site storage container rental"

Test phrase/exact variants and monitor actual search-term reports. Exclude irrelevant intents such as jobs, shipping freight, overseas cargo, free containers, DIY plans, container homes, auctions and purchase/sales searches if not offered. Do not claim estimated clicks/CPC before Keyword Planner and actual campaign geo/bids. Keyword Planner may need Ads account setup with billing details.

**Second test:** Facebook/Instagram image or short-video awareness, then a quote CTA. Segment by use case rather than vague broad audience. Don't assume Marketplace allows service listings; check its current category rules first.

**Geography:** Start near Leesport and areas with feasible delivery and favorable margins. User-reported **250 miles** is a planning ceiling for some cases, not public "we serve within 250 miles" guarantee. Google Ads can use radius or selected cities/ZIPs; Google Business Profile works differently. Paid ad targeting tied to delivery should normally emphasize people in your actual service locations, then review leads beyond them individually.

### Unit economics and lead-quality reporting
Measure *qualified requests*, *quotes delivered*, *won rentals*, *actual contribution*, *delivery feasibility* and *ad spend*. Avoid likes as the primary success metric.

A qualified request = named interested business/person with legitimate proposed use + ZIP/service location + approximate size/quantity + rental timeframe, after privacy and consent requirements are implemented.

Simplified economics:
- **Rental contribution** = customer payments collected − container sourcing/amortized cost − delivery AND pickup − handling/maintenance/damage/insurance allocation − other incremental costs.
- **Acquisition break-even per won rental** must not exceed positive incremental contribution; require an added buffer for profit.
- **Lead-to-customer rate** = won rentals / qualified inquiries.
- **Allowable cost per qualified lead** ≤ acquisition budget per rental × lead-to-customer rate.
- **Actual cost per qualified lead** = tracked campaign cost / qualified inquiries.
- No numerical CPA/CPC/ROI can be responsibly forecast from current evidence.

The operations-concept.html page has a *sample* company-vs-carrier delivery comparison; those values are illustrative and do not describe Noll's real economics.
