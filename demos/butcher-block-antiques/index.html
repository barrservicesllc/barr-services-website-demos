WHAT YOU MIGHT FIND

Curated, not cluttered.

Visit Lebanon Valley describes the shop as a curated stop for primitives, vintage collectibles, art, furniture and unique finds. Visitor feedback also points to glassware, local collectibles and practical home pieces.

01

Primitives & country

Weathered wood, utilitarian forms and pieces with the patina that makes an old object feel lived-in rather than manufactured.

02

Furniture & statement pieces

From smaller accent pieces to furniture that can anchor a room, public tourism coverage highlights furniture as part of the shop’s mix.

03

Glassware & table pieces

Visitor coverage specifically calls out glassware and practical pieces such as dishes and table linens—items meant to be used, not just displayed.

04

Collectibles, art & oddities

The shop’s appeal is the hunt: locally themed collectibles, decorative art, vintage accents and the occasional thing you did not know you were looking for.

WHY THE NAME WORKS

A shop with some history of its own.

Public visitor commentary notes that the building itself was once a butcher shop—part of the story behind the name Butcher Block Antiques. That is exactly the kind of detail an owner-approved site can turn into memorable local character.
