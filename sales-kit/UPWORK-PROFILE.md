## Profile overview

I build practical digital systems for small businesses and operators who need finished work, not vague consulting.

My current work spans polished small-business websites, AI-assisted automation, Python/Windows utilities, GitHub/repository cleanup, research and data workflows, prototype tools, QA/testing, and local AI/LLM integrations.

I work best on projects where the goal is concrete: launch a website, automate a repetitive process, repair or organize a repository, build a proof-of-concept, validate a workflow, turn research into a usable report, or connect existing tools into a dependable process.

## Proposal positioning

Open with the client's problem, then state the specific outcome you can deliver. Avoid generic lines about being passionate or hardworking.

Suggested opening:

> I can handle this as a concrete deliverable rather than an open-ended consulting project. Based on your description, I would first lock the inputs and success criteria, build the smallest working version, test it against the real workflow, and hand over both the finished result and the instructions needed to run it.

For website jobs:

> I build the actual page first and optimize around the business goal—calls, inquiries, visits or bookings—rather than starting with a generic theme. My recent local-business work includes auto repair, HVAC, salons, retail and food-service demos built as complete sales-ready concepts.

- For unknown-scope debugging or integration work, use the $45/hour rate or a paid discovery milestone.
