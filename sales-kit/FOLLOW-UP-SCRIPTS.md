# Follow-Up Scripts

Keep these short. The demo link does the selling.

## Same day — owner saw the demo

> Hey, this is Devon with Barr Services. Thanks for taking a look at the website concept I built for you today. Here’s the direct demo: [DEMO LINK]. If you want it turned into your actual site, I can replace the demo pieces with your real photos/info and launch it under your domain.

## Owner was not there

> Hey, I stopped by today because I built a website concept specifically for [BUSINESS]. I missed you, so here’s the demo: [DEMO LINK]. No obligation — I’d just like you to see what I made before I do anything else with it.

## 24-hour follow-up

> Just making sure the demo link opened correctly: [DEMO LINK]. If you like the direction, the next step is just confirming your real photos, services, hours, and domain. I can handle the technical side.

## Three-day follow-up

> Checking back on the [BUSINESS] website concept. If the design is useful but the timing or price isn’t right, tell me that too — I’d rather know where it stands than keep bothering you.

## Seven-day close-the-loop

> I’m closing out my follow-up list for this round. Do you want me to keep [BUSINESS] in the launch queue, circle back later, or close it out for now?

## “Send me information”

> Absolutely. The fastest summary is: your demo is already built here — [DEMO LINK]. A one-time launch is $495, or I can launch/manage it for $295 setup + $79/month. Domain cost is separate at cost. I’ll confirm every business fact with you before anything becomes official.

## “Facebook is enough”

> Facebook is useful, but it doesn’t give you full control over what somebody sees when they search your business. The website gives you one clean link for your services, phone, directions, photos, and business information — and Facebook can point to it instead of replacing it.

## “I need to ask my partner/spouse”

> No problem. Send them the demo link. I don’t need a decision today. If they have changes they want, write them down and I can tell you what is included before you commit.

## “I need to think about it”

> That’s fine. What part are you deciding on — whether you want a site at all, the design, or the price? I can leave you alone on the parts you already like and answer only the part that’s holding it up.

## “Too expensive”

> Understood. I don’t want to remove the parts that actually make it useful, but I can scope it down. If you only want the finished site launched with your information and no ongoing management, that’s the one-time option.

## After a yes

> Great. I’ll send you the short intake list so I can confirm the exact business information and photos. Once that’s approved, I’ll make the production version and connect the domain. Nothing from the demo becomes an official claim until you approve it.
