# Barr Services — Simple Website Options

Use this as the default pricing sheet unless Devon chooses to change a number before quoting.

## Option 1 — Launch

**$495 one-time**

For a business that wants the finished demo turned into its real website.

Includes:
- Replace demo wording with owner-approved business information
- Add owner-supplied photos/logo or production-safe licensed imagery
- Confirm services, hours, phone, address, and calls-to-action
- Mobile/desktop setup
- Domain connection
- HTTPS/SSL setup on the chosen host
- One owner revision round before launch
- Basic title/description/search metadata
- Final launch check

**Domain registration/renewal is billed separately at actual cost.** No paid hosting is required when the approved site fits a free static-hosting setup.

## Option 2 — Managed

**$295 setup + $79/month**

Includes everything needed to launch, plus:
- Barr Services keeps the site maintained
- Small text/hour/contact changes
- Link and form checks
- Up to 30 minutes of routine edits per month
- Basic uptime/deployment troubleshooting
- One point of contact instead of the owner managing GitHub/hosting

Month-to-month after launch. Domain cost remains separate.

## Option 3 — Growth

**$895 setup + $129/month**

For a business that wants the site actively used as a local sales asset.

Includes Managed, plus:
- Analytics setup
- Local search/metadata cleanup
- Google Business Profile link consistency review
- Seasonal offer or announcement updates
- Up to 60 minutes of routine site edits per month
- Quarterly conversion/content review

No ranking, traffic, lead, or revenue result is guaranteed.

## Close language

If they ask, “How much?”

> The demo is free to look at. If you want me to turn it into your actual website, the simplest launch is $495. If you want me to keep managing it after launch, I can lower the setup to $295 and handle it for $79 a month.

If price is the only objection:

> I already did most of the design work, so I can keep this simple. Tell me what part you actually need and I’ll scope only that instead of selling you extras.

## Payment default

For one-time Launch work: **50% to start, 50% before production launch/domain cutover.**

For Managed/Growth: setup fee + first month before production work begins, then monthly billing.

Never collect money for a domain without putting the registration in the client's name/account or documenting who owns it.
